Boundary coverage and signs
The four cases partition by ordered comparisons B<=A, B<=Y, X<=Y; the interior
case splits into the two closed regions numerator>=D*Z and numerator<=D*Z.
These cover the max cap, including equality. Zero costs and all ties use one

division occurs on the excluded beta=0 or X=Y boundaries. Primal and prior
