Integer reconstruction
Write the prior with common denominator D_Aand integer weights w, and the
mixed upper with denominator D_Pand weights z. For adaptive tree t define
A_tq=sum_x w_x 1{q paid by t at x}; for input x define
U_xq=sum_s z_s 1{q paid by priority tree s at x}. If the anchor is A_0,
the oriented lower constraints have integer normals A_t-A_0and the upper
constraints have normals D_P*A_0-D_A*U_x. Their suppressed denominators
are strictly positive. Divide each nonzero normal by its positive gcd,
never reversing its sign. Rows with all components nonnegative are redundant
on the nonnegative price orthant and may be omitted from the effective cone.
Normalize, sort and deduplicate cones only within the SAME source. Retain
one original case and anchor as a realizing certificate for every cone, plus

row sets define identical regions; no geometric equivalence beyond that is

Price-order fan and exact edge subdivision
For each permutation pi, the ordered-price cone

The exact nonnegative decomposition uses coefficients kappa_pi0and the

A certificate cone containing all four generators of a simplicial cone
contains that entire cone by linearity. Otherwise a branch may replace
an edge with a new ray w=primitive(a*u+b*v), a,b>0. The two child cones
replace u or v by w, keeping the other generators. They exactly cover the
parent: in alpha*u+beta*v, compare alpha/aand beta/band subtract the smaller
multiple of a*u+b*v. Other generator coefficients remain nonnegative.
Primitivizing w rescales its generator by a positive number and changes no
cone. Child generators remain independent when parent generators are.

The producer chooses subdivisions from a boundary inequality of a retained
cone containing the parent generator-sum point, splitting an edge with
opposite signs. This is only a search rule. Its termination/completeness is
NOTassumed. A coverage result is accepted only if every one of the24price
order roots has a finite, acyclic, exact subdivision proof whose leaves are
contained in explicitly identified SAME-source certificate cones. The
checker validates the arithmetic, split identity, generators, root fan and
leaf inequalities. Node/depth/time caps leave coverage unresolved.
