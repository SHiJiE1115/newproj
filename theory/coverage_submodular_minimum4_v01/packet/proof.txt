Exact mathematical result in the declared complete-source model
Literature priority and natural application gain are not established

1. The statement

In the complete binary equivalence-partition-fibre model, let R_A and R_P
be minimum worst-input expected actual purchased-set costs of zero-error
policies with input-independent private randomness. In P each seed fixes
one global query permutation, shared across answer branches, while allowing
causal permanent SKIPs and stopping. Both classes receive the same source,
fee and purchased answers. A fixed dual prior is not an assumed population.

The minimum number of indexed pair queries permitting R_A<R_P is exactly4
for normalized nonnegative monotone submodular fees, and for nonnegative
weighted coverage fees. The same minimum holds under strictly positive
nonempty fees and strict increase under every proper inclusion. An upper
witness already uses strictly positive individual query charges and strictly
positive pairwise shared-setup charges, without a global setup charge.
Its fee is strictly submodular on every incomparable pair of query sets.

On the fixed source below, this simple fee has exact values
  R_A = 125/7,       R_P = 161/9,       R_P-R_A = 2/63.
The ratio is1127/1125on this one cost point, not a global extremal bound.
This settles the previously open minimum for these cost classes within
the stated model. It does not change the additive minimum interval5--7,
and it is not a full classification of all four-query sources or fees.

3. A fee made of positive query costs and pairwise sharing

For a purchased set S, define the witness fee C by
  C(S)=sum_{i in S}c_i + sum_{i<j}w_ij * 1{S intersects{i,j}},
where
  c=(3,1,1,1),
  (w01,w02,w03,w12,w13,w23)=(2,1,2,4,4,4).
Each shared setup is paid once if either associated query is bought.
Activation itself reveals no answer; the information actions remain the
four original pair tests. The same charges apply to both policy classes.

For masks0through15, the fee table is
  (0,8,11,17,10,17,17,22,11,17,18,22,17,22,20,23).
It is a sum of ten positive weighted coverage indicators. Each such
indicator is normalized, monotone and submodular; summing proves these
properties for C. Adding a new query increases its individual charge
by at least1, while all setup increments are nonnegative. Thus C is
strictly increasing under every proper inclusion and positive on every
nonempty set. No zero-cost probe or negative charge is used.

More explicitly, for incomparable S,T,
  C(S)+C(T)-C(S union T)-C(S intersection T)
     = sum_{i in S\T, j in T\S}w_ij >=1.
The two set differences are nonempty and every pair weight is positive.
Comparable sets have the unavoidable equality. The least proper marginal
is1, the least nonempty fee is8, and the least incomparable slack is1.

7. A full-dimensional robust submodular neighborhood

Let G(empty)=0 and max_{S nonempty}|G(S)-C(S)|<=r. A whole policy's
cost on each input changes by at most r, because the fee is charged once
on its actual purchased set. The explicit A mixture and full-P dual give
  R_P(G)-R_A(G) >= 2/63-2r.
For r=1/126, this is at least1/63. At the same radius the least nonempty
fee is at least8-r>0, every proper marginal at least1-2r>0, and every
incomparable submodular slack at least1-4r>0. Comparable-set inequalities
are identities. Thus the entire15-dimensional normalized real-fee box is
strictly positive, strictly increasing and strictly submodular, with a
strict A/P gap. It need not consist entirely of coverage functions.

Within the ten positive individual/pair-setup parameters, perturbing every
parameter by at mostdelta changes each set fee by at most10delta. The
closed parameter box delta=1/1260 remains positive, consists of coverage
fees, and has gap at least1/63by the same bound. These conservative radii
are analytic consequences, not sampled fee grids or largest-region claims.

8. The exact minimum

Accepted V21 Theorem18 proves equality for every complete source with at
most3queries and every finite normalized nonnegative monotone fee. Both
coverage and submodular costs, and their stricter positive/increasing
subclasses, are contained in that class. The four-query complete witness
above supplies the matching upper size. Hence each stated minimum is4.
V21 Theorem5 gives a consistent stronger lower for arbitrary partial tasks,
but that broader scope is not needed for this complete-source conclusion.
No old three-query catalogue or hosted run was repeated in this step.
