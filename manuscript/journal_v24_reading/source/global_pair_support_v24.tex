\section{The pair-resource minimum over all complete four-query sources}
\label{app:global-pair-support}
The sourcewise exclusion in Appendix~\ref{app:pair-resource-support}
can be extended to every complete source. This changes the source
quantifier while keeping the same observations, final-set charges and
full causal-skip priority class. The finite proof below treats the
entire nonnegative price orthant, rather than a price sample.

\begin{theorem}[Universal one-pair equality and the four-query resource minimum, computer-assisted]
\label{thm:global-pair-resource-minimum}
Let $W_0,W_1$ be nonempty, disjoint projections of two complete
equivalence-partition fibres, with unrestricted class count, on a menu
$Q$ of at most four indexed pair-equality queries. For finite weights
$c_i,w_e\geq0$, charge
\begin{equation}\label{eq:global-pair-fee}
 F(S)=\sum_{i\in S}c_i+
       \sum_{e\in\binom Q2} w_e{\bf1}\{S\cap e\ne\varnothing\}.
\end{equation}
If at most one distinct pair has positive weight, then
$R_{\mathcal A}(W;F)=R_{\mathcal P}(W;F)$.
The values use input-independent private randomness, zero error on
every promised input, and worst-input expected actual final-set cost.
Each priority seed may choose its own global order and make causal
permanent skips.

Over all complete sources with exactly four indexed queries and fees
of the form~\eqref{eq:global-pair-fee}, the least number of positive
distinct pair weights permitting $R_{\mathcal A}<R_{\mathcal P}$ is
exactly two. The same minimum holds when all individual charges are
strictly positive.
\end{theorem}
Repeated occurrences of one pair combine into one weight. Each shared
group here has size exactly two; its activation supplies no extra
information. There is no separate global setup term. The minimum is
over complete four-query sources, not over arbitrarily large menus or
arbitrary resource groups. In particular, an additive gap already
exists at seven queries.

\paragraph*{Source cover and monotone majority.}
Use the complete four-query source cover and policy normalization in
the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:four-additive}. Endpoint SAME components
and DIFFERENT filters give all $465$ nonempty one-sided supports from
$4140$ endpoint partitions. The $42864$ ordered disjoint tasks form
$1356$ orbits under simultaneous coordinate permutations and label
exchange. These are complete physical sources; no literal answer
complementation is used as a source symmetry.

For $S\subseteq T$, each coefficient in
\[
 F(T)-F(S)=\sum_i c_i\bigl({\bf1}_{i\in T}-{\bf1}_{i\in S}\bigr)
  +\sum_e w_e\bigl({\bf1}_{T\cap e\ne\varnothing}
                         -{\bf1}_{S\cap e\ne\varnothing}\bigr)
\]
is nonnegative. Thus the fee is finite, normalized, nonnegative and
monotone. The earlier $1300$ certificate-threshold cases, $31$
same-input policy-subset cases and $14$ structural cases already prove
equality for every fee in this larger monotone class. This closes
$1345$ disjoint source orbits for all one-pair parameters. It uses the
mathematical scope of those certificates, not agreement at sampled
prices. Their full finite certificate outputs were also freshly
replayed in the combined execution described below.

\paragraph*{Closed continuous-parameter certificates.}
For each source and each of the six pairs, use the $24$ closed
individual-price orders. If $o$ is an order, write
\[
 c_{o(j)}=d_0+\cdots+d_j\quad(0\leq j<4),\qquad
 \lambda=d_4,\qquad d\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^{5}.
\]
Every path fee is a homogeneous linear form in these five variables.
The price-order orthants cover all nonnegative $c,\lambda$ including
ties, zero prices and zero setup charge. Each root is closed by the
same two finite-game mechanisms proved in
Theorem~\ref{thm:pair-resource-minimum}.

At a pure-saddle leaf, one original hard input lower-bounds every
full adaptive column by an anchor fee, while a legal original
priority column is bounded by that anchor on every original input.
At a dominance/lifting leaf, removing a weakly dominated minimizer
column or maximizer row preserves the finite mixed minimax value.
Following the deleted-row chains gives a common no-cheaper surviving
row for every terminal mixture. Every remaining column then has an
original priority replacement whose cost is no greater on
\emph{all original inputs}. This last condition includes removed
objective rows and all zero-prior correctness inputs. Replacing the
terminal mixture with the same seed weights gives
$R_{\mathcal P}\leq R_{\mathcal A}$. Policy inclusion gives equality.
Separate dominance runs on the two classes are not assumed to have
the same terminal game.

A split on a linear form $f$ uses both closed halves $f\geq0$ and
$f\leq0$. For parent generators $p,n$ with opposite signs, the
crossing vector $f(p)n-f(n)p$ lies on their shared boundary and is a
nonnegative combination of $p,n$. Together with the retained
same-sign and zero generators, these vectors generate the respective
halfcones. All cones lie in the nonnegative orthant and are pointed.
The reader checks full dimension five. A feasible nonzero ray is
extreme exactly when its active normals in the complete accumulated
inequality description have rank four. Removing only nonextreme
candidate rays consequently preserves the entire cone. Exact
Gauss--Jordan elimination over fractions reconstructs the clipped
cones and ranks separately from the producer's primitive-integer
elimination. A generator inequality therefore holds on the whole
cone, rather than only at tested cost vectors.

Across all $11\cdot6\cdot24=1584$ roots there are $1776$ nodes and
$96$ splits. All $1680$ leaves close: $887$ by pure saddles and
$793$ by dominance and full-original-row lifting. The separate readers
check $55742$ column deletions, $3913$ row deletions, $2829$
full-original-row priority lifts, $2704249$ scalar inequalities and
$3275$ exact ranks. Every node is reached once, all roots close and
there is no unstarted root or residual leaf. No mixed-template leaf
is needed. These checks, together with the full-policy and closed
cone-cover arguments, prove one-pair equality on every residual
source for the full continuous nonnegative domain. The $1345$
monotone cases and transport complete the four-query exclusion.

\paragraph*{Smaller menus and the upper witness.}
For fewer than four queries, add fresh record pairs forced DIFFERENT
under both label hypotheses. Give each dummy query zero individual
charge and no membership in a positive pair group. Every original
physical world extends by fresh classes, so the dummy answers are
constant zero. Removing known-answer dummy purchases preserves all
original observations and fees. Deleting them from each seed's global
order is a legal permanent skip. Thus both minimax values are
unchanged by padding, and the four-query exclusion covers every
smaller menu. A menu without any eligible pair has additive fees and
is also covered by Theorem~\ref{thm:four-additive}.

On the complete four-query source of
Theorem~\ref{thm:pair-resource-minimum}, take
$(c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3)=(4,3,2,3)$ and $w_{12}=w_{23}=2$, with all other
pair weights zero. That theorem gives the full-class values
$R_{\mathcal A}=286/29$ and $R_{\mathcal P}=79/8$, with gap $3/232$.
The combined scope check freshly recomputes the $40+32$ matching dual
inequalities and both all-input upper mixtures; it does not introduce
a new fee, prior or numerical solve. At most one positive pair is
excluded universally above, and this two-pair witness has strictly
positive individual charges. The minimum is therefore exactly two
in both the nonnegative and positive individual-charge classes.
Corollary~\ref{cor:sparse-pair-resource-robust} still gives a
six-resource box of radius $1/1856$ with gap at least $3/464$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph*{Interpretation and limits.}
The theorem strengthens the source quantifier of
Theorem~\ref{thm:pair-resource-minimum}; it does not change that
theorem's sourcewise witness or improve its approximately $0.13\%$
cost reduction. It does not classify every four-query submodular
fee, every two-pair instance, larger setup groups, all specified-prior
Bayes games, or larger query menus. Cost sharing, coverage modeling,
finite minimax certificates and cone clipping are established tools.
The candidate contribution is the exact universal pair-support
boundary under the stated complete-source and comparator contract.
The bounded comparisons with shared-cost testing~\cite{harris2025},
Pandora's reward-search objective~\cite{pandora} and stochastic
Boolean evaluation~\cite{hellerstein2022adaptivity} do not establish
literature priority or exclude all possible preserving reductions.
This is internal computer-assisted verification using different
programs from one research assistant, without external independent
implementation review, human author approval or formal proof-kernel
certification. No practical academic-evaluation advantage follows.
